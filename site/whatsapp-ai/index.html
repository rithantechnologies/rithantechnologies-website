07 / Common questions

Before you connect your business.

How do I sign up for Rithan AI?

Open Sign up, enter your business details and choose a password. Verify your email using the private link we send. Your account is then created in a paused workspace; our team reviews it before manual activation. You connect your own WhatsApp Business account only after the required authorization and setup checks.

Does this work with my current WhatsApp number?

It depends on your number's current setup and eligibility for Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform or supported coexistence options. We'll assess the connection route before promising that you can retain a particular app setup.

Do I give Rithan ownership of my WhatsApp account?

No. The business should control its own Meta business assets and approve its own connection. We assist with the configured integration and onboarding steps.

Will it reply in Tamil and Tanglish?

We can configure and test responses for Tamil, English and Tanglish. Final quality depends on the business information, supported workflows and testing with real examples.

Can it send promotions to all my contacts?

Promotional messages require recipient opt-in, appropriate approved templates and compliance with Meta's rules. Campaign capability is separately scoped; we do not offer unrestricted or unsolicited bulk sending.

Can a person take over a conversation?

We can design a human handover process for your setup. The available shared inbox, roles and routing depend on the chosen package and integration scope.

Can you connect this with my website or CRM?

We can assess supported website, lead-management, catalogue or API integrations and provide a separate scope based on your current system and the information that needs to move between services.

Are there setup or WhatsApp messaging charges?

Yes, a one-time setup fee may apply. Meta's messaging charges and any external services are additional where applicable. We'll share a scope-specific estimate before activation.
