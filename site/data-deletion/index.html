User Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Users of Rithan Link DM may request deletion of information associated with their connected Instagram account.

Disconnect Your Instagram Account

You may first disconnect Rithan Link DM from your Instagram account through the application's connection settings or through the permissions/settings provided by Instagram or Meta.

Request Data Deletion

To request deletion, contact Rithan Technologies through the contact information available at https://rithantechnologies.com.

Please include enough information to identify the connected Instagram account, such as:

  • Instagram username
  • The name of the service: Rithan Link DM
  • A statement requesting deletion of associated application data

Do not send passwords, Instagram access tokens, or other account credentials.

What We Delete

After a valid deletion request is verified, we will delete application data associated with the connected Instagram account that we are not legally required to retain.

Processing Time

Deletion requests will be processed within a reasonable period after identity and account ownership have been verified.

Questions

For questions regarding deletion or privacy, contact Rithan Technologies through rithantechnologies.com.
