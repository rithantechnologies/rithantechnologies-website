Rithan AI Data-Deletion Instructions

Effective date: 15 October 2026

If you would like us to review or delete personal information processed by Rithan AI, you can contact us directly. You do not need a Rithan AI login to submit a request.

1. Send a deletion request

Email rithantechnologies@gmail.com with the subject Rithan AI Data Deletion Request.

Request data deletion by email

To help us locate the correct account or conversation, include whichever of these details are relevant:

  1. Whether you are a WhatsApp customer, a staff member, or the business owner.
  2. The name of the business you messaged or operated through Rithan AI.
  3. The WhatsApp phone number associated with the conversation, if known, and an approximate date of contact. Include only information needed to find the record.
  4. Whether you want the entire available record deleted or are requesting a narrower correction, access request or deletion.

Do not email passwords, access tokens, verification codes, government identity document numbers, payment card details or other unnecessary sensitive data.

2. Verification and business ownership

We may need to confirm that you are authorized to request deletion, using an appropriate verification method, before modifying records. If a business uses Rithan AI to manage its customer conversations, we may coordinate with that business to address requests concerning data it controls. You can also contact the relevant business directly.

For business administrators, we may need to confirm account ownership and whether any account, financial or compliance records must be preserved.

3. What a request may cover

Depending on the service used and the verified request, Rithan AI data may include WhatsApp message copies and metadata in our systems, customer contact and lead records, AI reply drafts, staff replies, associated conversation history, staff account information, and business settings or knowledge supplied to the service.

After verification, we will review the available records and delete information that is no longer necessary to retain, subject to applicable requirements. Some information may be retained temporarily in backups, security or audit records, or where required by law. We will explain material limitations when responding to a request.

4. What this does not delete

Deleting records in Rithan AI does not automatically erase messages from another person's WhatsApp app, WhatsApp or Meta systems, a connected business's separate systems, or other service providers' records. You may need to make separate requests to those parties.

Disconnecting a WhatsApp Business integration or uninstalling WhatsApp does not by itself submit a deletion request to Rithan AI.

5. Processing and follow-up

We review requests after receiving enough information to identify the relevant account or conversation. Time needed depends on the scope of the request and any necessary verification or legal checks; we will respond using the contact information you provided.

If you believe a request has not been addressed, reply to the original email thread or contact us again at rithantechnologies@gmail.com.
