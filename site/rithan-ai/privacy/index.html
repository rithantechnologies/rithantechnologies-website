Rithan AI Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

This policy explains how the Rithan AI WhatsApp customer-support and business-inbox service handles information when a business uses our software or a customer communicates with a business through a connected messaging channel.

1. Who operates this service

Rithan AI is a software service offered under the Rithan Technologies brand, operated by PERIYASAMY, sole proprietor of DATAWINGS TECHNOLOGIES. Registered business address: No.130/6A-1, Harsha Towers, Thiruvika Road, Karur, Tamil Nadu 639001, India. Service location: Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu, India.

For privacy questions, contact rithantechnologies@gmail.com. See also our Rithan AI data-deletion instructions.

2. Information we process

  • Customer communications: WhatsApp identifiers and phone numbers, message text, message IDs, timestamps, message-type information and relevant messaging or delivery metadata. Where applicable, business staff may record conversation notes, enquiries or lead details supplied during a chat.
  • Business accounts: Contact details for a subscribed business, authorized staff email addresses, role and session information, connected WhatsApp Business Account and phone identifiers, configuration, and the knowledge or FAQs that business provides.
  • Service records: AI-generated reply drafts, staff replies, approval and delivery states where enabled, conversation handoffs, lead or appointment information where configured, usage information and operational security logs.
  • Messages about privacy requests: Contact and identification details voluntarily provided to help us locate and handle a request.

Do not send sensitive identity documents, passwords, access tokens, OTPs or payment-card information through ordinary WhatsApp messages unless the receiving business specifically requires and securely supports that information.

3. Why we process this information

We process information to deliver customer-support inboxes, route enquiries to the correct business, produce tenant-specific AI reply drafts, manage staff handoffs and follow-ups, support any features a business enables, investigate technical issues, prevent abuse, maintain security and meet applicable legal obligations.

AI-generated content may be reviewed or controlled by authorized business staff. Automation and sending capabilities depend on the connected business's settings and the available messaging integrations. AI responses can be inaccurate and should not replace professional advice.

4. Businesses and their customers

Each business using Rithan AI manages its own customer relationships and determines how it uses conversations and customer details. We provide the supporting software and process information to deliver that service. Business owners and their authorized staff can access conversations associated with their own business; the system is designed to keep business accounts separated.

If you are a customer of one of these businesses, you may contact that business directly about how it uses your information. You may also contact us using the email above for assistance.

5. AI processing and service providers

To generate or classify replies, relevant customer message text, questions and business-supplied knowledge may be transmitted to OpenAI through its API. WhatsApp messages and messaging metadata are also processed by Meta/WhatsApp as part of the WhatsApp Business Platform. Our hosting, database and infrastructure providers may process information as needed to operate the service. Where configured, email delivery services may process business notification details.

We do not sell personal information or WhatsApp conversation data to advertisers. Use of these third-party services is also subject to their applicable terms and privacy policies. Service providers may process information in jurisdictions outside your own.

6. Storage, security and retention

We store service records in access-controlled infrastructure, use role-based access for staff, and apply safeguards intended to protect account credentials and communication endpoints. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

We retain conversation records, business configurations, operational records and support information while needed to provide the service, address security or support issues, or comply with applicable obligations. The retention period may differ depending on the record and business arrangement. The current service does not promise a universal automatic deletion period. Some records may remain temporarily in backups or where legally required.

7. Your choices and requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of information relating to you, subject to applicable law and appropriate identity or account verification. Business customers may request deletion of their connected Rithan AI data. WhatsApp chat deletion on a personal device or deletion of data held by Meta is separate from deletion of records held by Rithan AI.

To request deletion, follow our data-deletion instructions or email rithantechnologies@gmail.com. Do not include passwords or one-time verification codes.

8. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy to reflect changes to Rithan AI, its integrations or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page will be updated when we publish material changes.

Scope: This page applies to Rithan AI. The separate Rithan Technologies / Rithan Link DM privacy policy continues to describe the existing Instagram automation service.
