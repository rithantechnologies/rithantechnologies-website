06 — Questions

Before you message us.

Can I see something before I pay?

Yes. We build a working sample of your own site using your real products and send you a link. Don't like it, you owe nothing and we delete it.

How long does it take?

A launch site is about two weeks from the day your photos and details are ready. Bigger builds run four to eight weeks. The hold-up is almost always content from your side, not the code.

Do I need to know anything technical?

No. We handle the domain, hosting, SSL, email and Google setup, and show you how to update it in a short walkthrough.

Who owns the website?

You do. Domain and hosting are registered in your name and the code is handed over on delivery. If you ever leave, you take everything with you.

Do you work outside Coimbatore?

Yes — across India and the Gulf. Everything from the first message to handover works over WhatsApp, email and video.
