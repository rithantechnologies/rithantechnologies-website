Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

Rithan Technologies operates Rithan Link DM, a service that helps Instagram Professional account owners automate responses to comments and send configured links through Instagram messaging.

Information We Process

When an Instagram account is connected to Rithan Link DM, we may process information made available through the Instagram API, including Instagram account identifiers, usernames, media identifiers, comments, comment identifiers, messaging events, and access tokens required to provide the service.

How We Use Information

We use this information only to provide features requested by the account owner, including detecting configured comment keywords, responding to comments, sending private replies, managing automations, and maintaining service reliability.

Access Tokens

Instagram access tokens are used only to communicate with Meta's Instagram APIs on behalf of the connected account. We do not sell access tokens or use them for advertising purposes.

Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers required to operate our infrastructure, or disclosed when required by applicable law.

Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security, comply with legal requirements, and resolve technical issues.

Data Deletion

Users may request deletion of data associated with their connected Instagram account. Instructions are available at: https://rithantechnologies.com/data-deletion.

Third-Party Services

Rithan Link DM integrates with Meta and Instagram. Use of Instagram is also subject to Meta's and Instagram's applicable terms and privacy policies.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures intended to protect information processed through our services.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or data deletion requests, contact Rithan Technologies through the contact information provided on rithantechnologies.com.
